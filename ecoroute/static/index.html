    How EcoRoute protects answer quality
    
        
      
          
    1. Shadow evaluations. Blind side-by-side grading against the baseline. Their energy is logged as overhead and kept out of the savings.
      2. 
          
    2. Cascade. Each answer gets a quick check for clear failures: empty, cut off, refused, far too short, or missing code. A failed answer goes to the next stronger model, and both attempts are counted.
      3. 
          
    3. Feedback. ▼ on an answer can re-ask one tier up. After three votes, a model's thumbs-down rate lowers its quality score in routing.
      4.

    Does price track energy? Wh per 1k output tokens vs. output price, log–log
    
        
    
        
    Open-weight models (filled) get a compute-based estimate from active parameters. Closed models (hollow) are estimated from price, on the idea that price reflects GPU time, and GPU time is energy.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    How EcoRoute decides
    
        
      
          
    1. Score the prompt. Interpretable features (reasoning, code, maths, system design, requested length…) give a 0–1 complexity and a tier from 1 to 5.
      2. 
          
    2. Filter. Drop models that are under-powered for that tier, unavailable, or break your constraints.
      3. 
          
    3. Estimate impact. Energy (Wh), carbon (grid gCO₂e/kWh), water (on-site cooling + off-site generation) and cost for each candidate.
      4. 
          
    4. Rank. Weighted score of sustainability, cost, quality and latency for the chosen policy. Over-powered models get no extra quality credit.
      5. 
          
    5. Run & measure. Call the winner (falling back on errors), measure real tokens, log everything against a baseline.
